Finding deals & staying safe

How to spot a good deal

Compare the live price to the launch / list price on this site. Prefer official stores (Steam, PSN, Xbox, Epic, Humble, GOG). A deep discount on a keyshop is not automatically better if the key is region-locked or from a risky seller.

Protect yourself with third-party keys

Grey-market keyshops can be cheaper but may void support, use stolen payment methods, or sell unauthorised keys. Check seller ratings, refund policy, and whether activation is for your region. When unsure, pay a bit more on Steam / PSN / Epic.

Physical & used (CeX, eBay, Marketplace)

Used discs are often cheaper. Check condition grades, seller feedback, and return windows. Facebook Marketplace is link-only here — meet safely and prefer in-person payment methods you trust.

What this tool is not

We do not sell games, process payments, or guarantee any listed price. Data comes from public sources and can be wrong, delayed, or blocked. Always confirm on the store before you buy.
