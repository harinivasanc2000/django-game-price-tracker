About this tracker

Personal tool to compare public game prices (Steam, multi-store PC, UK shops) and track drops. Not a store. Prices can be wrong or blocked — always confirm on the seller site.

Pages

Home Search Deals Guide Research Settings History

  • Home — popular titles + live Steam specials
  • Search — Steam keyword search + autocomplete
  • Detail — multi-store compare, chart, track, prediction heuristic
  • Guide — public “what to buy & where”
  • Research — offline ML plan + CSV export

Keyboard

  • / — focus search (anywhere except inside an input)
  • Esc — close tracked drawer

For developers

  • Read DEVELOPER.md in the repo root (file map + “I want to change…” table)
  • Edit popular games: apps/games/constants.py
  • New store client: apps/games/clients/
  • Changelog: always prepend at the top of CHANGELOG.md
  • Exports: /export/tracked.json, /export/training.csv, /health/

Safety

  • Prefer official stores when prices are close
  • Keyshops / grey market can be cheaper but riskier
  • Author is not responsible for purchases or losses
