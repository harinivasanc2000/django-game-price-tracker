Architecture (planned)

Website shows live public prices + optional precomputed scores. Your machine runs training (scikit-learn, XGBoost, PyTorch, etc.).

  • Collect → public APIs + polite search scrapes (title, price, URL, rating only).
  • Store offline → SQLite / Parquet / CSV under a local data/ folder.
  • Train offline → notebooks or scripts on your Mac.
  • Publish artefacts → e.g. ml/predictions.json later for display only.

What we already collect (product-only)

  • Product title, price, currency, product URL
  • Public aggregate rating when on the card
  • Store name, kind (official / retail / used / third-party)

Not collected: seller personal identity, addresses, phones, emails, private messages.

Data sources

Suggested local workflow

  1. Track games; run python manage.py refresh_prices daily.
  2. Download training CSV from this page.
  3. Join Kaggle metadata on steam_app_id.
  4. Label: “dropped 15%+ within 30 days?” from future rows.
  5. Train offline; keep metrics + predictions JSON local until you choose to display them.

Ethics

  • Rate limits + cache; keyshops stay labelled risky.
  • Predictions are not financial advice.
  • No private seller PII or non-public sales data.
